Pixie Bookhouse — Read what you love

Founded by Ma. Vanessa B. Fanoga out of a lifelong passion for reading, Pixie Bookhouse is an independent bookstore dedicated to making books accessible and encouraging reading as a life-changing hobby.

Operating for seven years, Pixie Bookhouse offers guaranteed authentic, pre-loved, and affordable books to readers while actively giving back to the community, working to spread the joy of literacy, one book at a time.

About Pixie Bookhouse

Pixie Bookhouse is an independent, community-focused, secondhand bookstore. Every pre-loved book we offer is guaranteed authentic and fairly priced, making quality reading accessible to everyone.

Our Founder

Pixie Bookhouse was founded by Ma. Vanessa B. Fanoga, whose lifelong passion for reading inspired the shop's mission to share the joy of books with a wider community.

What We Offer

Authentic, pre-loved, and affordable books. Transparent book condition. Reasonable pricing. A curated selection for readers of every kind.

Our Mission

To make books accessible, to encourage reading as a life-changing hobby, and to give back to the community — spreading the joy of literacy, one book at a time.
